FAQ
Before we start.
Price, payment, scope and what you get at the end.




How do I start a commission?
Send me a message on Discord with your idea, the game it’s for and any references. We agree on what to build and when before any code is written.


How much does it cost?
It depends on the complexity: the size of the system, how many screens and animations it has, the data it saves and your deadline. Tell me what you need and I’ll send you a quote.


How can I pay?
PayPal, Robux or PIX. A revenue share, a percentage of what the game earns, is also possible. We can talk about it.


What kind of projects do you take?
Anything in Roblox: maps and builds, outfit maps and catalogs, shops and purchase flows, gameplay systems, animated interfaces wired to real code, VFX, DataStore and Studio tools. Discord bots and APIs too.


What do I get at the end?
The finished system, organized and tested in Studio, ready for your game. If an issue shows up after launch, I fix it.
